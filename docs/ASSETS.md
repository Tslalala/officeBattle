## 生成提示词

所有素材统一使用 **hand-painted pixel-art-inspired illustration, crisp painterly edges, muted navy teal and amber palette, no readable text, no UI, no watermark**。各素材的主题提示如下：

1. `office-day.png`：Empty modern Chinese office interior at 5:42 PM; wide straight-on side view; three desks, large window with warm skyline, bulletin board, clock, vacant fourth workstation.
2. `office-night.png`：Same office after 6 PM; dark purple skyline, fluorescent flicker, unsettling shadows and vacant fourth workstation.
3. `characters.png`：Four distinct full-body Chinese office workers isolated on a transparent background: Lin Tang in cream cardigan, supervisor Zhou in charcoal blazer, new employee in blue shirt, ghostly Chen Xu in gray clothes.
4. `clues.png`：Eight distinct objects in a 2 × 4 grid on transparent background: folded handwritten note, scratched employee badge, registration form, keycard, stamped envelope, drawer, analog clock, pen.
5. `hallway-title.png`：Empty office hallway at dusk leading to a warm open door; abandoned employee badge on floor; dark negative space for title.
6. `meeting-room.png`：Empty modern Chinese office meeting room at dusk; long table, eight chairs, one pushed away, glass wall, blank projector screen.
7. `break-room.png`：Empty office pantry at night; vending machine, water dispenser, coffee cups, small table and red light from doorway.
8. `records-room.png`：Office records archive after hours; filing cabinets, stacked boxes, one open drawer, single desk lamp lighting a missing employee folder.

透明立绘与道具目前作为整张图由 CSS 定位使用，原图完整保留，便于后续制作精确切图与动画。
